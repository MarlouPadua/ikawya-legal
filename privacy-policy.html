Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ikawya, developed by Marlou Padua, handles information when you use the ikawya mobile application.

ikawya is designed around personal reflection, self-learning, journaling, conversations, and keeping information about yourself on your device.

1. Information Stored by ikawya

ikawya may allow you to create and store information such as:

  • Personal facts and information you choose to teach the app
  • Names, nicknames, preferences, interests, and other personal information you enter
  • Conversation messages
  • Diary entries and notes
  • Photos and images that you choose to add
  • Audio recordings or audio files that you choose to add
  • Information used to help the app learn your preferred words, phrases, and response patterns
  • App settings and preferences
  • Local activity and history information
  • Local backup and export data

This information is primarily stored locally on your device using the app's local storage.

2. Information Collected or Transmitted

ikawya does not operate a developer-hosted account system or require you to create an account in order to use its core features.

Based on the current implementation, personal facts, conversations, diary content, locally stored images, recordings, learned patterns, and local settings are not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated server.

However, certain features can communicate with third-party internet services.

Link previews and embedded content

When you enter or use a web URL in features that support link previews or embedded content, ikawya may send that URL to the relevant website or third-party service to obtain information needed to display a preview or embedded content.

Depending on the URL, this can include services associated with platforms such as:

  • YouTube
  • Vimeo
  • Dailymotion
  • SoundCloud
  • Spotify
  • X/Twitter
  • TikTok
  • Flickr

For websites that support oEmbed or similar mechanisms, ikawya may also access the webpage or an embedding endpoint to obtain the information required for the preview.

The network request is associated with the URL you provided. ikawya does not intentionally send your entire diary, conversation database, personal facts, or local audio files to these services merely because you use a link preview.

External links and embedded media

Some links may be opened using external applications or displayed using embedded web or media components. When this happens, the relevant third-party service may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

You should review the privacy policies of third-party services when using their content.

3. Microphone and Audio

ikawya requests microphone access because the app provides audio recording functionality.

When you record audio, the recording is stored locally within the app's storage on your device.

ikawya does not automatically upload recorded audio to a developer-operated server.

Audio may become part of an export or backup if you explicitly choose a feature that includes audio. If you then share that exported file with another application or service, the recipient of that file may process the information according to its own policies.

4. Photos and Images

ikawya may allow you to select or add images using your device's image-selection functionality.

Images you add to the app are stored locally and may be associated with facts, conversations, diary entries, or other local content.

ikawya does not automatically upload those images to a developer-operated server.

Images may be included in an export or backup when you explicitly choose to export or back up them.

5. Backups and Exports

ikawya provides local backup and export functionality.

Backup files are created from information stored on your device. The current implementation encrypts backup/export content before saving the backup file.

Backups are file-based and are not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated cloud server.

If you choose to share an exported backup using your device's sharing functionality, the file may be transferred to an application, person, or service that you select. Once you intentionally share a file outside ikawya, the receiving party's privacy practices apply.

6. How Information Is Used

Information stored in ikawya is used to provide the app's features, including:

  • Displaying your personal information and facts
  • Maintaining your conversations
  • Supporting self-reflection and journaling
  • Providing locally generated responses
  • Learning your preferred words and response patterns
  • Maintaining your local history and settings
  • Creating local backups and exports
  • Displaying link previews and embedded content when you request or use those features

ikawya does not use your personal content for advertising purposes.

7. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of ikawya does not intentionally include advertising or a developer-operated analytics system.

The application code reviewed for this release does not contain the commonly used advertising or analytics services such as Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, Sentry, Mixpanel, Amplitude, AppsFlyer, Adjust, or OneSignal.

Third-party websites or media services accessed through link previews, embedded content, or external links may have their own analytics, advertising, or tracking practices. Those practices are controlled by the respective third parties.

8. Data Sharing

ikawya does not sell your personal information.

ikawya does not automatically share your locally stored personal content with the developer.

Information may leave your device in the following circumstances:

  1. Link preview or embedded-content requests — when you use a URL-based feature, the relevant URL may be sent to the corresponding third-party website or service.
  2. External links or media — when you choose to open or view third-party content.
  3. User-initiated exports or backups — when you explicitly choose to share an exported or backed-up file with another application, person, or service.

User-initiated transfer of personal information is different from ikawya automatically selling or distributing your information.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because ikawya primarily stores user-created information locally on the user's device, the developer does not maintain a central copy of that information.

Local information remains on your device until you delete it through available app functionality, remove the relevant content, remove application data, uninstall the application, or otherwise overwrite or delete the local files.

Exported or backed-up files may continue to exist wherever you have saved or shared them. You are responsible for deleting copies of exported or shared files from locations you control.

If you intentionally send an exported file to another person or service, you may need to contact that recipient directly to request deletion of their copy.

10. Data Security

ikawya is designed to keep personal information primarily on the user's device rather than on a developer-operated server.

The application uses Android's application-private storage and local database mechanisms for locally stored information.

Backup and export files created by the application are encrypted before being written as backup/export files.

No security method can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their device, operating-system account, and any exported backup files from unauthorized access.

11. Children's Privacy

ikawya is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated online service.

Because the application primarily stores information locally, parents or guardians should supervise a child's use of the application where appropriate.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted in a manner that raises a privacy concern, please contact us using the information below.

12. Third-Party Services

When ikawya accesses third-party websites, media platforms, oEmbed services, or external applications, those third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples of third-party platforms that may be accessed through supported link or media features include:

  • YouTube
  • Vimeo
  • Dailymotion
  • SoundCloud
  • Spotify
  • X/Twitter
  • TikTok
  • Flickr

ikawya does not control the privacy practices of these third parties.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

You should periodically review this Privacy Policy for changes.
